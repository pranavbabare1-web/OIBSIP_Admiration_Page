Person I Admire

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of the greatest scientists and leaders of India. He was known as the "Missile Man of India" and inspired millions of students with his thoughts and vision.

I admire him for his simplicity, dedication, and love for education. Even after achieving great success, he remained humble and focused on serving the nation.

Why I Admire Him

  • Hardworking and disciplined
  • Great leader and teacher
  • Inspired youth of the nation
